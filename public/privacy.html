🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  |  Skilldhan

🛡️ Skilldhan values your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase on our website, we collect the following information:

Information Purpose
Name Order identification and customer support
Email address Order confirmation and download access
Mobile number Order identification and customer support
Order and payment IDs Transaction records and verification
IP address (indirectly) Fraud prevention and rate limiting

2. How We Use Information

  • ✅ To process your order and provide download access
  • ✅ To confirm payments through Razorpay
  • ✅ To provide customer support
  • ✅ To prevent fraud and cyberattacks
  • ✅ To improve website performance through analytics
  • ❌ We do not sell your information to third parties

3. Cookies and Analytics

We may use the following tracking tools:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): To understand website traffic and user behavior. Google's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Meta Pixel: To measure advertising performance. Meta's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Session storage: Order details are stored temporarily in your browser and removed when the browser session ends.

You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings. Some website functions may be affected.

4. Data Security

🔐 We use industry-standard measures to help protect your information.

  • SSL/TLS encryption: Data is transferred over an encrypted connection.
  • Helmet.js and CSP: Protections against cross-site scripting (XSS) and other attacks.
  • Rate limiting: Helps prevent denial-of-service and brute-force attacks.
  • Razorpay payments: We do not store card or bank details; payments are processed by Razorpay.
  • Tokenized downloads: Downloads require a secure cryptographic token.

5. Payment Information

We do not store card details, UPI PINs, or banking credentials. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Read Razorpay's Privacy Policy: razorpay.com/privacy

6. Data Retention

  • Order records (name, email, phone, and order ID) are retained for 2 years.
  • Download tokens expire automatically after 72 hours.
  • Analytics data is retained according to Google and Meta policies.

7. Your Rights

You may have the following rights:

  • 📋 Access: Ask what information we hold about you.
  • ✏️ Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • 🗑️ Deletion: Request deletion of your information, subject to legal requirements.
  • 🚫 Marketing opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails.

To exercise these rights, email us at support@skilldhan.shop.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as needed. Significant changes will be posted on the website. The "Last updated" date appears above.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, contact us:
